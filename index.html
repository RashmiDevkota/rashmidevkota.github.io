There was a time when I was struggling, academically and within myself. I felt like I didn't have anything I was good at. Cricket changed that. It gave me an identity.

From my notebookScene 01

Lotus Is My Favorite Flower

An eight year old girl sat curled up on the sofa, an ice cream slowly melting in her hand, her eyes, ears, and heart somewhere else entirely. The television was playing Sony Max, a channel full of South Indian movies dubbed in Hindi. She didn't know how many flowers existed in the world, but that day she learned the name of one she would remember forever. Lotus.

It wasn't one movie that introduced her to it. It was two. Two different images, two different moments, and the same feeling each time. In the first, the hero held the heroine's hand tightly as she leaned toward a lotus that made no logical sense at all, a flower meant to bloom in water, standing improbably among icy mountains. Logic didn't matter. Feelings did. Trusting him completely, she stepped closer to the edge, reaching for it simply because it was her favorite. That was the moment the girl on the sofa found hers. Lotus, she said, and meant it.

She might have forgotten it with time, but childhood memories have a way of returning. Another movie, another scene. A hero dives into a deep pond to bring back a lotus, only because the woman he loves cherishes it. Watching this, the older version of that little girl finally understood. It was never the lotus itself. It was the way it was chosen. The way it was earned. The hero who risked everything to reach it, and the heroine who trusted him completely. That trust turned a flower into a feeling.

Even today, when I say lotus is my favorite flower, I know it carries more than beauty. It carries emotion. It carries trust. It carries a piece of childhood that never really fades. Honestly, it's like breathing. You never forget how.

My first book is on its way. Somewhere between these stories, a longer one is taking shape, and I can't wait to share it.
